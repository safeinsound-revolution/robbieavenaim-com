On ‘Shine Caught Blue’ Avenaim melds with his unique human / machine interface to draw intricate delicate textures from drums, bells and piano, while special guest Jim Denley contributes extraordinary flute. The result is an album of plaintive melancholy and dynamic tonal exploration, where moments of petrichor restraint swell into squalls of dextrous superhuman stick-work and sophisticated euphoric abstraction.

Limited pressing of 300 copies

File under: experimental acoustic / avant-garde / robotic percussion

The Federation Bells is an immersive public sound sculpture located in the Middle Terrace of Birrarung Marr, Melbourne, Australia. The instrument is a harmonically unique kinetic art installation comprising 39 inverted bells located in a riverside park. The bells were designed by Anton Hassell and Neil McLachlan in collaboration with Swaney Draper Architects, and were unveiled to the public in 2001.This instrument is essentially a computer-controlled carillon, with bells dispersed through a small field rather than confined to the traditional bell tower. The Federation Bells are distributed in a grid, and listeners are able to walk among the bells, inside the instrument. The bells are played via the sequenced control of highly dynamic metronomic hammers designed to mimic human touch.
